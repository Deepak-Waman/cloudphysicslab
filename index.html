About the project

Science first. Open by design.

Cloud Physics Lab

An evolving educational platform for students, early-career researchers, and anyone curious about the physics of clouds. Future modules can connect foundational concepts to published research, atmospheric observations, and numerical modelling.

Created by Dr. Deepak Waman

Postdoctoral researcher in cloud microphysics at KIT, Germany. The long-term roadmap includes advanced microphysics lessons, research tools, Marathi-language science content, and visualizers for particle size distributions, CAPE/CIN, and cloud processes.
